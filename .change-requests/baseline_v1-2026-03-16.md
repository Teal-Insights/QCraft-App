# Change Request: baseline_v1 — Oracle Employment Growth Claim

## What I Expected (per oracle)

The oracle (`planning/oracles/baseline_v1.md`) states:
> "Employment growth equals working-age population (WAP) growth for **all years**,
> including the WEO period."

## What I Found (in golden master)

The golden master proves this claim is **wrong for early WEO years (2009-2021)**.

During years 2009-2021, employment growth = production function residual:
```
emp = (real_gdp_growth/100 - productivity/100) / (1 + productivity/100) * 100
```

Example: Year 2010
- WAP growth: 15692/15169*100-100 = 3.448%
- Residual: (7.484-2.196)/(1.02196)*100 = 5.175%
- Golden master: 5.175% (matches residual, NOT WAP)

Starting at year 2022 (WEO_MAX_YEAR - 7), employment switches to WAP growth.
From 2023+ (WEO_MAX_YEAR - 6), both formulas give the same result due to
the productivity back-calculation making the identity hold.

## Proposed Fix

Update `planning/oracles/baseline_v1.md` Phase 1 to clarify:
- Years < WEO_MAX_YEAR - 7: employment = residual from GDP growth and WDI productivity
- Years >= WEO_MAX_YEAR - 7: employment = WAP growth

## Status

Worked around — implementation matches the golden master exactly.
